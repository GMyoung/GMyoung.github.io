The opening uses the earlier yellow-green paint and floating brush fragments.
Unlock requires 7.2 seconds of motion and 1.1 turns. The progress bar shows
the accumulated gesture, which can resume after a pause. The cloth catches
lightly at first and gains traction as it is pulled taut.
The yellow-green paint rotates with separate momentum. It turns more slowly
on a light pull, speeds up on a hard pull, and coasts when the hand stops.
Desktop wheel and touchpad scrolling can advance the same opening.
The V, E and Y pieces follow the gaps in the source drawing. Turning
assembles each letter, which then flies away before the next one starts.
At unlock the full wordmark returns once with a green trail and the title cheer.
The title uses a short recording of children cheering. The writing room loops
Charlie Huang's quiet silk-string guqin recording of Pei Lan. Foundation
lessons reveal the licensed Hanzi Writer Data outlines along their medians.
Foundation and name lessons retain dry ink. The free-writing water control
sets the flow mode for new strokes. Car Game is on the homepage.

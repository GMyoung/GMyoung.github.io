# Showcase scenery credits

MODARIS is a non-commercial educational project. The scenes below were made by other artists and are used under the licence each of them chose. Files are in `static/modaris/showcase/`. Each was scaled and placed as background scenery; lighting was replaced with unlit colour, and textures were resized to 1024 px and stored as WebP.
